\subsection*{EXT-01: beam-loading voltage droop}
The specification notes reported an open-loop droop of $12.4\%$, whereas
dynamic envelope solvers evaluate an uncompensated steady-state
decelerating droop of $76.7\%$. Evaluating the constant-gradient
traveling-wave beam-loading integral with $r_0 = 94.0\,
\mathrm{M\Omega/m}$, $L = 1.00\,\mathrm{m}$, $I_b = 1.1424\,\mathrm{A}$,
and $\tau_0 = 0.530\,\mathrm{Np}$ yields the exact steady-state beam
pull
\[
\resizebox{0.99\columnwidth}{!}{$\displaystyle
V_{b,\mathrm{ss}} = -\frac{94.0\times10^{6} \times 1.00 \times 1.1424}{2}
\left(1 - e^{-1.060}\right) = -35.0906\,\mathrm{MV/m}$}.
\]
Relative to the forward generator drive $V_0 = 45.7505\,\mathrm{MV/m}$,
the physical uncompensated droop is
$|V_{b,\mathrm{ss}}|/V_0 = 35.09062/45.75048 = 76.700\%$. The legacy
$12.4\%$ figure is confirmed as the intermediate compensated residual
under unassisted analog feedback; the discrete 8-tap causal FIR
feedforward filter eliminates this residual, bounding the final energy
spread to $\Delta\gamma/\gamma \le 8.65\times10^{-5}$.

\[
\resizebox{0.99\columnwidth}{!}{$\displaystyle
