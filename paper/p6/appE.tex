\section{Coffin--Manson Thermo-Elastic Fatigue and Yamamura Sputtering}
CVD diamond armor ($k_{\text{th}} = 2000$~W/m$\cdot$K, $E = 1050$~GPa,
$\alpha_{\text{th}} = 1.1\times10^{-6}$~K$^{-1}$, $\sigma_f' =
2500$~MPa, $\epsilon_f' = 0.010$, $b = -0.08$, $c = -0.60$) sustains
$\Delta\sigma = 359.3$~MPa under the $4.375$~MJ flash, giving
$N_f = 9.85\times10^{13}$ cycles.  Paris integration from
$a_i = 10~\mu$m gives $N_{\text{crack}} > 10^{10}$ cycles.  Yamamura
sputtering yields for $\mathrm{He}^{2+}\to$W span
$2.14\times10^{-2}$ (1~keV) to $4.92\times10^{-2}$ (10~keV); the
$+50$~kV deflection grid at $\eta_{\text{shield}} = 99.5\%$ bounds slat
erosion to $0.0118$~mm/yr.
