\label{subsec:mhd3d}

    r_{c,\mathrm{3D}} = r_{c,\mathrm{1D}} \left(\frac{16}{3}\right)^{1/3}
    = 0.8631 \times 1.7477 = 1.508~\text{m},
\end{equation}
resolving the GATE-43 reconciliation: the 1D isotropic bound
$0.8631$~m underestimates the dynamically compressed 3D flux volume by
the factor $(16/3)^{1/3}$.  The fireball stagnates at $r_c = 1.508$~m
inside the $R_{\text{chamber}} = 2.20$~m vessel, preserving a
$\Delta r_{\text{cushion}} = 0.692$~m magnetic cushion
($> 0.50$~m limit).
