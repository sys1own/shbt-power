\subsection{PIC sheath potential, Child--Langmuir ceilings, and the
$-50$~kV suppressor saddle}
\label{subsec:pic_sheath}

The $(r,z)$ cylindrical PIC solver advances the 24.14~MA,
$2.0~\mu\text{s}$ alpha burst across the three Venetian-blind stages
($0.8$, $1.8$, $2.7$~MV; $0.35$~m gaps) with a relativistic Boris pusher
in Strang splitting and an $O(N)$ multigrid Poisson resolution of the
sheath potential.  Guiding-center $\mu$-conservation across the
$5.0 \to 0.05$~T trumpet sets the collected beam diameter to
$D_{\text{beam}} = 5.000$~m --- the active collection footprint within
the $12.0$~m cryostat vacuum boundary (GATE-30 reconciliation).
Thermionic electron injection at $n_{e,\text{inj}} = 5.937 \times
10^{17}$~m$^{-3}$ ($T_e = 25$~eV) clamps the effective current density
to $J_{\text{eff}} \approx 308~\text{A/m}^2$, below the modified
Child--Langmuir ceiling $J_{\text{CL}} = 579.59~\text{A/m}^2$, while the
design stage currents ($4.5$, $15.2$, $28.0$~A/m$^2$) retain a
$\sim 10\times$ margin against virtual-anode formation.  Sternglass--Joy
secondary emission from the refractory slats is quenched by the
$-50$~kV suppressor grid, whose saddle-point barrier
$e\Delta\Phi \ge 20$~keV prevents electron back-acceleration into the
collection anodes.
