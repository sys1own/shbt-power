\subsection{Empirical Metrology, GUM Covariance, and Discrepancy
Reconciliation}
\label{subsec:metrology_reconciliation}

The 1,325~$\text{MW}_{\text{th}}$ enthalpy recovery balance is
calibrated in situ by $\text{Ni}_{80}\text{Cr}_{20}$ Joule substitution
heaters ($R_{\text{cal}} = 50.000 \pm 0.005~\Omega$) across a five-tier
10--50~MW ramp, enforcing the enthalpy-substitution slope
$|\hat{\beta}_1 - 1| \le 0.0020$.  Propagating the full JCGM~100:2008
covariance matrix over $\mathbf{x} = [\dot{m}, \Delta T, \bar{C}_p,
\dot{Q}_{\text{leak}}]$ with the empirical cross-correlation
$r(\dot{m},\Delta T) = +0.1355$ gives
\begin{equation}
u_c^2(P) = 15.3920~\text{MW}^2 \;\Rightarrow\; u_c(P) = 3.923~\text{MW}_{\text{th}},
\end{equation}
and $U = k\,u_c = 7.846~\text{MW}_{\text{th}}$ at $k = 2$ ($\pm
0.599\%$ of the $1{,}309.995$~MW delivered baseline).
