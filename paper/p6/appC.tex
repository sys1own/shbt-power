Strang splitting: half-kick by $\mathbf{E}$, rotation in $\mathbf{B}$,

$-50$~kV suppressor saddle ($e\Delta\Phi \ge 20$~keV).  Thermionic

579.59~\text{A/m}^2$, and Sternglass--Joy slat secondaries are
