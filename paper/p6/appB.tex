\section{Multi-Channel Breit--Wigner Ladder, Solbrig Kernel, and
Stewart--Pyatt Continuum Lowering}
The power6 solver advances five $^{12}\mathrm{C}^*$ compound levels
($E_x = 2.124$, $4.439$, $8.921$, $16.106$, $16.570$~MeV) with radiative
and alpha partial widths, broadened by the Solbrig transmission kernel
\begin{equation}
\resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{$\displaystyle
\sigma_{\text{eff}}(E,T) = \frac{1}{\Delta\sqrt{\pi}}
\int_0^\infty \sigma(E')\frac{\sqrt{E'}}{\sqrt{E}}
\left[e^{-(\sqrt{E'}-\sqrt{E})^2/\Delta^2}
- e^{-(\sqrt{E'}+\sqrt{E})^2/\Delta^2}\right]dE'
$}
\end{equation}
with $\Delta(E,T_{\text{eff}}) = \sqrt{4m_aEk_BT_{\text{eff}}/(m_a+m_T)}$
and the Debye-spectrum effective temperature $T_{\text{eff}}(0) =
\tfrac{3}{8}\Theta_D = 69.375$~K for $\Theta_D = 185$~K decaborane.
Stewart--Pyatt continuum lowering
\begin{equation}
\resizebox{\columnwidth}{!}{$\displaystyle
\Delta I_z = \frac{(z+1)e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0 a_i^*}
\left[\tfrac{3}{2}\frac{(1+(a_i^*/\lambda_D)^3)^{2/3}-1}
{(a_i^*/\lambda_D)^3}\right]
$}
\end{equation}
pressure-ionizes the solid prior to thermal heating.
