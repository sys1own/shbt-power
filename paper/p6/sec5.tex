\subsection{Pulse-periodic thermo-mechanics, PWI erosion, and
supercritical-helium CHT}
\label{subsec:pp_thermo}

Each 100~Hz shot deposits a $4.375$~MJ Bremsstrahlung flash
($q'' \approx 2.87$~MW/m$^2$ over $152.4$~m$^2$) into the first-wall
armor.  CVD diamond plates ($k_{\text{th}} = 2000$~W/m$\cdot$K,
$\alpha_{\text{th}} = 1.1 \times 10^{-6}$~K$^{-1}$) cap the surface
excursion at $\Delta T_{\text{surf}} = 279.9$~K, bounding cyclic stress
to $\Delta\sigma_{\text{surf}} = 359.3$~MPa $< \sigma_y = 2.0$~GPa and
yielding a Coffin--Manson fatigue life $N_f = 9.85 \times 10^{13}$
cycles ($> 10$~yr continuous duty).  Yamamura--Eckstein sputtering of
tungsten collector slats by the $1$--$10$~keV ion tail
($Y = 4.92 \times 10^{-2}$ at 10~keV) drives $2.358$~mm/yr unshielded
erosion; the $+50$~kV electrostatic deflection grid
($\eta_{\text{shield}} = 99.5\%$) attenuates this to
$0.0118$~mm/yr.

The $10.0$~MPa supercritical-helium loop circulates $\dot{m} =
450$~kg/s across the 300~K$\to$900~K envelope.  Churchill-correlated
Darcy friction over the primary micro-channel cold plate
($D_h = 1.50$~mm, $f = 0.0192$, $\Delta p = 0.185$~MPa) and the
collector-slat secondary array ($D_h = 3.20$~mm, $f = 0.0175$,
$\Delta p = 0.097$~MPa) sums to a combined $\Delta p = 0.282$~MPa,
requiring $W_{\text{pump}} = \dot{m}\Delta p/(\rho_{\text{avg}}
\eta_o) = 14.22$~MW $\le 15.0$~MW of parasitic circulator power.
