\section{3D Dedner GLM-MHD System and Non-Linear MRT Flute Saturation}
The conserved state $\mathbf{U} = (\rho, \rho\mathbf{u},
\mathcal{E}, \mathbf{B}, \psi)$ evolves under HLLC--Rusanov fluxes with
Dedner GLM cleaning at hyperbolic speed $c_h$: $\partial_t\psi =
-c_h^2\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B}$, $\partial_t\mathbf{B} +
\nabla\cdot(\mathbf{u}\mathbf{B} - \mathbf{B}\mathbf{u} + \psi
\mathbb{I}) = 0$.  The generalized Ohm's law retains anisotropic
Spitzer--H{\"a}rm resistivity and the Hall drift term.  The $13.125$~MJ
fireball stagnates at $r_c = 1.508$~m (the 3D $(16/3)^{1/3}$
flux-compression scaling of the 1D $0.8631$~m bound), preserving the
$0.692$~m cushion against the $2.20$~m wall.  Magnetic-shear and FLR
stabilization saturate all flute modes $m = 2,\ldots,64$ at
$h_{\text{spike}} \le 0.177$~m $< 0.180$~m.
